Tips

Wrong answers are never punished. The touched answer fades away and the right one glows, so every turn ends in success. If nothing is touched for a while, the instruction is repeated and the right answer glows.

To stop the player leaving the game by accident: on iPhone/iPad use Guided Access (Settings › Accessibility › Guided Access). On Android use App pinning (Settings › Security › App pinning).
